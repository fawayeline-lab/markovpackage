# Sources

Date accessed: 4 November 2026. References are numbered [S1], [S2]… and cited in the text.

## Sources actually consulted

- **[S1]** Wikipedia, "Markov chain". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_chain
  Used for: Markov property, stochastic matrix, π(n) = π(0)Pⁿ, stationary distribution, irreducibility, aperiodicity, recurrent and transient states, absorbing state, application fields (queues, PageRank, finance).
- **[S2]** Wikipedia, "Little's law". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little%27s_law
  Used for: statement L = λW and its conditions (stationary and ergodic system).
- **[S3]** Wikipedia, "Queueing theory". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queueing_theory
  Used for: definition of queueing theory, link with operations research, Kendall notation, history (Erlang).
- **[S4]** Web search results on PageRank: Brin & Page (1998), "The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine", *Computer Networks and ISDN Systems*, 30, pp. 107-117. The reference was confirmed through a search engine; **the text of the paper was not read**.

## References cited by the sources above (not read directly)

- **[R1]** J. D. C. Little (1961), "A Proof for the Queuing Formula: L = λW", *Operations Research*, 9(3), 383-387. doi:10.1287/opre.9.3.383. Original reference of Little's law, found in [S2].
- **[R2]** A. K. Erlang, early work on queues (1909, 1917), found in [S3] and [S1].
- **[R3]** Page, Brin, Motwani & Winograd (1999), PageRank, cited in [S1].
- **[R4]** Trivedi (2002), *Probability and Statistics with Reliability, Queueing, and Computer Science Applications*, cited in [S1] for the link between Markov chains, reliability and queues.

## Recommended book for further reading (existence checked, content not re-read here)
